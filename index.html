          How to use it

          Four steps

          1. Open Shipment Prediction and pick Country From, Country To and Food Type. Start typing and choose from the list — typing ind offers India and Indonesia.
          2. Set transit time, number of legs, package type and fill ratio.
          3. Press Predict. Nothing is scored until you do, so you can change your mind freely while filling the form.
          4. Read the risk band, the shelf-life check for your food type, then What if to see how a shorter trip, fewer hand-offs or other packaging changes it.

          The risk model only uses four inputs known before shipping. Sensor readings such as temperature and humidity are only known after the trip, so they are not model inputs — for live weather and hazards, use the Overview page, which reads Open-Meteo and GDACS directly.
